About this data: Figures come directly from the BLS Local Area Unemployment Statistics (LAUS) program, the same source Minnesota DEED uses for its county unemployment tool. Data is not seasonally adjusted. Estimates are produced monthly using a model-based approach combining Current Population Survey data, unemployment insurance records, and Census Bureau estimates.  •  Counties: Dodge, Fillmore, Freeborn, Goodhue, Houston, Mower, Olmsted, Rice, Steele, Wabasha, Winona.
